Why This Initiative

Why Orvia Exists

Motive

Everyone should have the opportunity to grow together. Useful knowledge deserves to be shared, and learning should never stop.

Keep Learning. Keep Growing. Keep Moving Forward.

My Intention

I often wished someone had introduced me earlier to the things I am learning now. Instead of continuing to wait for that opportunity, I decided to create the kind of learning space I once wished existed.

Goal

I may not be able to provide complete knowledge about everything, but my goal is to help participants develop a different perspective and become more curious about learning beyond the session.
